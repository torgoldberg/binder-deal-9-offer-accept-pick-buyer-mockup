5. Decisions locked by this mockup

Replacement, not addition: sale-class Mark sold opens this sheet only. Non-sale types keep their existing confirm (or none). No intermediate “are you sure?” after Confirm — Confirm is the commit.

Single-select list: radio rows (avatar + display name + quiet “commented” meta). No prices, offers, or comment snippets in MVP — candidates are people, not threads.

Confirm gate: disabled until a candidate is selected, and while loading / empty / error / offline / accept-in-flight.

Navigation: success → DEAL-5 deal chat via returned conversation_id; do not invent a new room chrome.
